What it is

Our own CS2 tournament, open to the community. Sixteen teams, single elimination: lose a match and you're out. Every match is best of one, except the final, which is best of three.

Who can play

Any team of five, plus one sub if you like. The captain registers the team and talks to the admins. Every player should be on our Discord — that's where the bracket, match calls and server details are posted.
