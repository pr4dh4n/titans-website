What it is

The Titans Community Cup is our own CS2 tournament, open to the whole community. Sixteen teams play one night of single elimination. Lose a match and you're out. Every match is best of one, except the final, which is best of three.

Who can play

  • Any team of five, plus one sub if you like. You don't need to be in the clan.
  • Every player is 13 or older.
  • Every player has a working mic.
  • Every player is in our Discord. That's where check-in, match calls and admin help happen.

Where matches are played

Servers are announced on DiscordOur CS2 community servers aren't open yet. Matches are played on servers we announce on Discord before the cup, with the connect details sent to each captain.

Schedule

  1. Thu 15 OctSignups close.
  2. Fri 16 OctThe draw. The bracket goes up here and on Discord.
  3. Sat 17 Oct · 17:30 UTCCheck-in on Discord. Every team must check in.
  4. Sat 17 Oct · 18:00 UTCRound of 16 starts (Bo1).
  5. ThenQuarter-finals and semi-finals (Bo1). Each round starts as soon as the one before it is done.
  6. LastThe final (Bo3). The winners get the trophy and their Discord role.
The draw happens on 16 OctoberEvery slot shows TBD until then. Team names go up here after the draw.
  1. TeamsFive players and up to one sub, all 13 or older. Once the draw is done, you can't add or swap players.
  2. Discord and micsEvery player must be in our Discord and have a working mic. The captain speaks for the team with admins.
  3. Check-inCheck in on Discord 30 minutes before the cup starts (17:30 UTC). A team that doesn't check in loses its place.
  4. Map vetoMaps come from the current CS2 Active Duty pool. In a Bo1, captains take turns banning maps until one is left. In the Bo3 final, each team bans one, picks one, bans one more, and the last map left is the decider. A coin flip decides who starts.
  5. SidesA knife round decides who picks the starting side on each map.
  6. Be on timeIf your team isn't on the server 10 minutes after your match is called, you forfeit that match.
  7. DisconnectsIf a player drops, pause the game and tell an admin. You get up to 10 minutes per map to get them back.
  8. No cheatsNo cheats, scripts, macros or bug exploits. Breaking this gets your team removed from the cup and banned from future ones.
  9. RespectNo abuse, slurs or harassment of players, admins or casters, in game or on Discord. Admins can warn or remove players.
  10. Admins' calls are finalIf something isn't covered here, admins decide, and their decision is final.
