Custom Enchantments 2.0 is coming to Survival

Souls, armour sets, the Tinkerer and the Alchemist. Here's the gear system that will be waiting for you when Season I opens.

Titans staff · 1 Oct 2026

Season I opens on Friday 9 October at 19:00 UTC, and it opens with new gear. Custom Enchantments 2.0 is our own enchantment plugin, built by our team, and it will power every enchant on Survival.

Every enchant belongs to one of six rarities. The rarer the book, the more it can do.

SimpleUniqueEliteUltimateLegendaryFabled

The souls system

Put a Soul Tracker on your weapon and it will collect souls from your kills. Some of the strongest enchant levels will need souls to wake up. Use them, and they're spent.

Your blade remembers every fight.

Soul Gems will let you store souls and move them between weapons, so a lucky night of PvP is never wasted.

Scrolls and dusts

  • White Scroll protects an item if an enchant book fails.
  • Holy White Scroll keeps the item when you die.
  • Black Scroll pulls a random enchant off an item into a book.
  • Secret and Magic Dust raise a book's chance of working.
  • Transmog Scroll tidies the order of enchants on your item.

Tinkerer & Alchemist

Two new menus. The Tinkerer will trade your enchanted gear and spare books for XP. The Alchemist will combine two identical books into a higher level, or two dusts into a stronger one, for an XP cost. You'll open them with /ce tinkerer and /ce alchemist.

Armour sets

Wear all four pieces of a set to unlock its bonus. Two sets will be ready when Season I opens: Lava and Frost.

What's in 2.0

New

  • Souls system with the Soul Tracker and Soul Gem
  • Tinkerer and Alchemist menus
  • Lava and Frost armour sets
  • Mobs killed by players can drop enchant books
  • About 20 new ways for enchants to fire, including fall damage, fishing and elytra flight

Changed

  • Some enchant levels cost souls to activate
  • Enchants can require, or block, other enchants on the same item

Fixed

  • Damage from the Swordsman enchant counts as the player's own attack
