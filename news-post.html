Custom Enchantments 2.0: coming to Survival

Souls, armour sets, the Tinkerer and the Alchemist. Here's the gear system waiting for you when Season 1 opens.

Season 1 starts on Friday, October 9, and it starts with new gear. Custom Enchantments 2.0 is our own enchantment engine, built in-house, and it powers every enchant on the network.

The souls system

Attach a Soul Tracker to your weapon and it starts collecting souls with every kill, from players and mobs. Some of the strongest enchantment levels now need souls to wake up. Use them, and they're spent.

Your blade remembers every fight.

Soul Gems let you store souls and move them between weapons, so a lucky night of PvP is never wasted.

New items

  • White Scroll protects an item if an enchant book fails.
  • Holy White Scroll keeps the item on death.
  • Black Scroll pulls a random enchant off an item into a book.
  • Secret and Magic Dust raise a book's success rate.
  • Transmog Scroll tidies the order of enchants on your item.

Tinkerer & Alchemist

Two new menus in the Citadel. The Tinkerer trades enchanted gear and spare books for XP. The Alchemist combines two identical books into a higher level, or two dusts into a stronger one, for an XP cost. Open them with /ce tinkerer and /ce alchemist.

Armour sets

Wear all four pieces of a set to unlock its bonus. Two sets are ready for launch, Lava and Frost, with more to come during the season.

Full changelog

Added

  • Souls system with Soul Tracker and Soul Gem
  • Tinkerer and Alchemist menus
  • Lava and Frost armour sets
  • Mobs killed by players can drop enchant books
  • Around 20 new enchant triggers, including fall damage, fishing and elytra flight

Changed

  • Some enchant levels now cost souls to activate
  • Enchants can now require, or block, other enchants on the same item

Fixed

  • Damage from the Swordsman enchant now counts as the player's attack
