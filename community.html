Who keeps it fair

Staff team

Four staff roles keep the servers safe and friendly. Here's what each one does and how to reach them.

Owner

Runs the clan

Runs TheTitansClan: the servers, the store and the big decisions. Has the final say.

Open a support ticket on Discord and ask for the Owner.

Admin

Runs the servers

Looks after the servers and the staff team, and handles problems a Moderator can't solve.

Open a support ticket on Discord.

Moderator

Keeps it fair

Watches chat and games, checks player reports, and mutes or bans people who break the rules.

Report a player on Rules & Support, or open a ticket on Discord.

Helper

Answers questions

Helps new players: answers questions in chat and on Discord and points people to the right place.

Just ask in chat, in-game or on Discord. A Helper can fetch a Moderator if needed.

Staff names are posted at launch.
