Who keeps it fair

Staff team

Four roles keep the server safe and friendly.

Owner

Runs TheTitansClan: the servers, the store and the big decisions. Has the final say.

Open a ticket on Discord and ask for the Owner.

Admin

Looks after the servers and the staff team, and handles problems a Moderator can't solve.

Open a ticket on Discord.

Moderator

Watches chat and games, checks player reports, and mutes or bans people who break the rules.

Report a player, or open a ticket on Discord.

Helper

Helps new players: answers questions in chat and on Discord, and points people to the right place.

Just ask in chat. A Helper can fetch a Moderator if needed.

Staff names are posted at launch.
