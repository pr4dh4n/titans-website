The short version

  • Ranks and items are added to your account right after you pay.
  • Full refund if your item never arrived and we can't fix it, you were charged twice, or a child bought it without a parent's permission (parent tells us within 14 days).
  • Otherwise, once your item is delivered and used, the sale is final.
  • No refunds for unused time on a rank, or for ranks lost to a ban.
  • Always ask us first. A chargeback gets the rank removed and may get your account banned.

This page explains when you can get your money back for something you bought in our store, and how to ask. The store isn't taking payments yet. These rules will apply once it opens.

1How delivery works

Ranks and in-game items are digital. They're added to your account right after your payment goes through. If yours doesn't show up, rejoin the server first.

2When you get a full refund

You can get all your money back if:

  • Your item never arrived. It still isn't on your account 24 hours after you paid, and our support team can't fix it.
  • You were charged twice for the same order.
  • A child bought it without permission. A parent or guardian must contact us within 14 days of the purchase. The item is removed from the account when the refund is made.

3When a purchase is final

In other cases, once your item has been delivered and you've started using it, the purchase is final and we can't refund it.

4Ranks and season passes

A monthly rank lasts 1 month, and a season pass lasts until the season ends (about 3 months). If you stop playing part-way through, we don't refund the time left over.

5Bans for breaking the rules

If you're banned or muted for breaking the server rules, you don't get a refund for ranks or items you can't use while you're punished.

6Chargebacks

A chargeback is when you ask your bank or card company to take a payment back, instead of asking us. Please always talk to us first; most problems are quick to fix. If you make a chargeback without asking us, the rank or item is removed and your account may be banned.

7How to ask for a refund

  1. Join our Discord and open a ticket in #tickets. Pick Payments (parents can pick Parents).
  2. Include your receipt or order number and the Minecraft or Steam username the purchase was for.
  3. Tell us what went wrong.

We answer within a few days. If we agree to a refund, it goes back through our payment partner.

8Physical merch

If we sell physical merch, like shirts, you can return it unused within 14 days of it arriving. Open a Payments ticket first and we'll tell you how to send it back.

9Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page. The version that applies to your purchase is the one shown here on the day you paid.
