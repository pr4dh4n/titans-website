How to join

Checking server…·Season I opens Fri 9 Oct, 19:00 UTC

Versions 1.21 to 26.3. Takes about a minute.

  1. Open Minecraft Java EditionAny version from 1.21 to 26.3 works.
  2. Multiplayer → Add ServerName it anything, for example “Titans”.

Your first minutes

You land in the Titan Citadel, our spawn hub. Have a look around, then try these.

  • /rtpStart somewhere random in the wild.
  • /spawnCome back to the Citadel any time.
  • /kit citizenClaim your free starter kit.
  • /sethomeSave the spot you're standing on as home. /home takes you back.

Can't connect?

“Outdated client” or “Outdated server”
Your game version doesn't match. Use any version from 1.21 to 26.3 — you can pick the version in the Minecraft Launcher under Installations.
“Can't resolve hostname”
Check the spelling: it must be exactly mc.thetitansclan.com. Then try again — sometimes the internet just needs a second go.
Stuck on “Connecting…”
The server may be restarting. Check the status at the top of this page, or ask on our Discord.
