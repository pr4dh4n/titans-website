These terms apply when you join our Minecraft network, play on our CS2 servers, use our Discord, visit this website or buy something from our store. By doing any of those, you agree to them. We've kept them short and in plain English. If something here doesn't make sense, ask us.

1.Who we are

TheTitansClan is a community-run Minecraft network and CS2 clan. When these terms say “we” or “us”, they mean the team that runs TheTitansClan. When they say “you”, they mean you, the player.

We are not affiliated with, or endorsed by, Mojang Studios, Microsoft or Valve. Minecraft and Counter-Strike are their trademarks. When you play Minecraft on our servers, Minecraft's own EULA and usage guidelines still apply too, and the same goes for Valve's terms when you play CS2.

2.Accounts and age

  • You must be 13 or older to play on our servers or join our Discord.
  • If you're under 18, you need a parent or guardian's permission before you buy anything.
  • You're responsible for your own account. Don't share it, and don't let other people play on it. Anything done on your account counts as done by you.
  • Making a new account to get around a ban counts as breaking the rules.

3.Playing fair

Our server rules are part of these terms. They cover Minecraft, PvP, CS2 and Discord. Staff may warn, mute, kick or ban players who break them. The punishment guide shows what usually happens, and you can appeal if you think staff got it wrong.

4.Store purchases

The store isn't taking payments yet. When it opens, these rules apply:

  • A licence, not ownership. When you buy a rank or an item, you're paying for the right to use it on our servers. It isn't property you own, and it has no value outside our servers.
  • How long ranks last. A monthly rank lasts 1 month. A season pass lasts until the end of the season, which is about 3 months.
  • Prices are shown in Indian rupees (₹). Payments are handled by our payment partner, not by us directly.
  • Perks can change. We may change what a rank includes between seasons to keep the game fair and fun. We'll announce changes before a new season starts.
  • Refunds follow our Refund policy.

5.Punishments

If you break the rules, staff can remove your access to some or all of our servers, for a while or for good. If that happens, you don't get a refund for ranks or items you can no longer use.

6.Chargebacks

A chargeback is when you ask your bank or card company to take a payment back, instead of asking us. If there's a problem with a purchase, please open a ticket first. We'd much rather fix it. If you make a chargeback without talking to us, the rank or item is removed and your account may be banned.

7.No guarantees

We run our servers “as is”. We work hard to keep them online and fair, but servers can crash, go down for updates, lose progress or close. Game modes, worlds and features can change. We can't promise the service will always be available or free of bugs.

8.Changes to these terms

We may update these terms. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and we'll post about big changes on our Discord. If you keep playing after a change, you're agreeing to the new terms.

9.Contact us

The way to reach us is a ticket on our Discord. Open one in #tickets and pick the type that fits. Parents can choose the Parents ticket. See Contact staff for more.
