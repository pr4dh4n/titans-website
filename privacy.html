Draft policy. We're finalising these policies before the server launches. If anything is unclear, open a ticket on Discord.

The short version

  • We only collect what we need to run the servers, stop cheaters and handle purchases.
  • We never sell your information, and we don't show ads.
  • This website has no tracking cookies. Your cart is saved only in your own browser.
  • Logs are deleted after about 90 days.
  • Want a copy of your data, or want it deleted? Open a ticket on our Discord.

This page explains what information we collect when you play on our servers, talk to us or use this website, why we need it, and what you can ask us to do with it. We collect as little as we can.

1What we collect

  • Your Minecraft username and UUID (the ID number Minecraft gives every account), so we can save your progress, ranks and items.
  • Your Discord username when you join our Discord or open a ticket, so we can reply to you.
  • Your IP address in our server logs. We use it to keep the servers safe and to make sure banned players stay banned.
  • Chat logs from our servers, so staff can check reports and deal with abuse.
  • Purchase records from our payment partner: what you bought, when, and the order number. We never see your full card or bank details. The payment partner handles those.

2What this website stores

  • Your cart is saved in your own browser (in its “local storage”). It stays on your device and isn't sent to us.
  • No ads and no tracking cookies. We don't use advertising or tracking tools on this site.
  • The server status widget shows how many players are online. To get that number, your browser asks a public status service, mcsrvstat.us, about our server. Like any website, that service can see your IP address when your browser asks.
  • Fonts on this site are loaded from Google Fonts, so your browser also contacts Google's servers to download them.

3Why we use it

  • To run the servers and keep your progress, ranks and items.
  • To stop cheating, abuse and ban dodging, and to check player reports and appeals.
  • To deliver what you buy, and to sort out payment problems and refunds.
  • To answer you when you contact us.

We don't use your information for advertising.

4How long we keep it

  • Server logs and chat logs: about 90 days, then they're deleted.
  • Ban records: until the ban ends. For a permanent ban, that means we keep the record.
  • Purchase records: as long as we need them for our accounts.

5Who we share it with

We share purchase details with our payment partner, because they handle the payment. We don't share your information with anyone else, and we never sell it.

6Children

Our servers and Discord are for players aged 13 and up. If you're a parent or guardian and want to know what we hold about your child, or want it deleted, open a Parents ticket on our Discord and we'll help.

7Your choices

You can ask us for a copy of the information we hold about you, or ask us to delete it. Open a General help ticket on our Discord and tell us your Minecraft username. We may need to check the account is yours first.

Some things we may have to keep even if you ask, such as purchase records we need for our accounts, or the record of a ban that hasn't ended yet. If so, we'll tell you.

To clear your cart from this website, empty it in the store or clear this site's data in your browser settings.

8Changes to this policy

We may update this policy. When we do, we'll change the “Last updated” date at the top of this page, and we'll post about big changes on our Discord.

9Contact us

Questions about your data? Open a ticket in #tickets on our Discord. See Contact staff for the ticket types.
