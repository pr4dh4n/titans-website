The law of the land

Server rules

Short and clear. If you're not sure whether something is allowed, ask staff first.

These apply everywhere on the Minecraft network: the hub, Survival, Bedwars and every mode we add.

  1. No cheats or hacked clientsX-ray, kill aura, fly hacks, auto-clickers and similar mods are all banned.
  2. Respect everyoneNo hate speech, slurs, threats or harassment, in chat or anywhere else.
  3. No griefing or stealingDon't break, change or take anything from other players' builds or chests.
  4. No real-money tradingDon't buy or sell items, in-game money or accounts for real money.
  5. Don't exploit bugsFound a bug? Report it on Discord instead of using it.
  6. AFK machines only in the AFK poolDon't use machines or tricks to stay online while away, except in the AFK pool. AFK pool time doesn't count for playtime rewards.
  7. Staff decisions standThink a staff member got it wrong? Use the appeal form, not chat.

These apply wherever player fighting is turned on, including clan wars.

  1. No combat loggingDon't leave the game in the middle of a fight to avoid dying.
  2. No killing in safe zonesSpawn and other safe zones are for everyone. No attacking players there.
  3. No spawn-camping new playersDon't wait near spawn to pick off players who just arrived.
  4. No trappingDon't trap players at warps or homes in a way they can't escape.
  5. Win fairNo bugs, glitches or exploits to win a fight.
  6. Clan wars need two yesesA clan war only happens when both clans agree to it.

For our CS2 community servers (coming soon) and clan 10-mans.

  1. No cheats, scripts or macrosWallhacks, aimbots, movement scripts and macros are all banned.
  2. No ghosting or stream snipingDon't use a stream, a spectator or a friend to see where the enemy is.
  3. Keep voice and chat cleanNo toxic voice, slurs, spam or abuse in chat.
  4. No griefing teammatesNo blocking, team-flashing or damaging teammates on purpose, and no throwing rounds.
  5. Admins run the 10-mansFollow the admin's call on teams, maps, pauses and disputes.

For every channel and voice room on our Discord server.

  1. Stay on topicUse each channel for what its name says.
  2. No spam, self-promo or adsNo floods of messages, invite links or adverts for other servers.
  3. No NSFW or shocking contentNothing sexual, gory or upsetting, including in names and profile pictures.
  4. Don't DM staff about bansUse the appeal form and a ticket. DMs aren't how appeals work.
  5. English in public channelsSo everyone, including staff, can follow the conversation.

Appeal a ban

Everyone makes mistakes, staff included. If you think your ban or mute was wrong, or you've learned from it, tell us here.

  1. Fill in the form and press Copy my appeal. Your answers are copied, nothing is sent yet.
  2. On our Discord, open a ticket in #tickets, pick Ban appeal, and paste your answers.
  3. Staff read it and reply in your ticket. Keep an eye on it.
  • One appeal per ban. If it's turned down, you can try again after 7 days.
  • Be honest. Appeals with made-up stories are turned down.
  • Please don't DM staff or open extra tickets. It won't make it faster.

Questions & answers

How do I join on Java?
Open Minecraft Java (version 1.21 up to 26.3), choose Multiplayer, then Add Server. Type mc.thetitansclan.com as the address, press Done, then Join.
How do I join on Bedrock?
On phone or Windows, go to Play, then the Servers tab, scroll down and tap Add Server. Type mc.thetitansclan.com as the address and 19132 as the port, then save and join.
How do I join on a console?
Xbox, PlayStation and Switch don't have an Add Server button for outside servers, so you need a workaround to get in. Open a General help ticket on our Discord, tell us which console you have, and staff will walk you through it.
How long until my rank arrives?
The store isn't taking payments yet. Once it opens, ranks are added right after payment. If yours doesn't show, rejoin the server first. Still missing after 24 hours? Open a Payments ticket with your order number and username.
Can I get a refund?
Sometimes. You get a full refund if your item never arrived and we can't fix it, if you were charged twice, or if a child bought something without a parent's permission. Most other purchases are final once you've used them. The Refund policy has the details.
What's a season pass?
A season lasts about 3 months, and each one has its own medieval theme. A season pass gives you a rank for the whole season for one price, which costs less than paying monthly. For example, Squire is ₹99 a month or ₹249 for the season.
Does AFK time count for rewards?
No. Time spent in the AFK pool doesn't count towards playtime rewards. Only time you're actually playing does.
I'm under 18. Can I buy things?
Yes, but only with a parent or guardian's permission. If a child buys something without permission, a parent can open a Parents ticket within 14 days for a full refund (the item is removed).
How long do appeals take?
There's no fixed time. Staff read appeals in order and reply in your ticket. Opening extra tickets or messaging staff won't make it faster. If your appeal is turned down, you can appeal the same ban again after 7 days.
