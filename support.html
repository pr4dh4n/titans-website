The law of the realm

Server rules

Short and clear. Not sure if something is allowed? Ask staff first.

Last updated 4 October 2026

Everywhere on the Minecraft server: the Citadel, Survival, Bedwars and every mode we add.

  1. No cheats or hacked clientsX-ray, kill aura, fly hacks, auto-clickers and similar mods are all banned.
  2. Respect everyoneNo hate speech, slurs, threats or bullying, in chat or anywhere else.
  3. No griefing or stealingDon't break, change or take anything from other players' builds or chests.
  4. No real-money tradingDon't buy or sell items, in-game money or accounts for real money.
  5. Don't exploit bugsFound a bug? Report it to staff instead of using it.
  6. AFK machines only in the AFK poolDon't use machines or tricks to stay online while away, except in the AFK pool. Time in the AFK pool does not count toward playtime rewards.
  7. Staff decisions standThink a staff member got it wrong? Use the appeal form, not chat.

Wherever player fighting is turned on, including fights between clans.

  1. No combat loggingDon't leave the game in the middle of a fight to avoid dying.
  2. No killing in safe zonesSpawn and other safe zones are for everyone. No attacking players there.
  3. No spawn-camping new playersDon't wait near spawn to pick off players who just arrived.
  4. No trappingDon't trap players at warps or homes in a way they can't escape.
  5. Win fairNo bugs, glitches or exploits to win a fight.
  6. Clan wars need two yesesA clan war only happens when both clans agree to it.

For the Community Cup, clan 10-mans and our community servers when they open.

  1. No cheats, scripts or macrosWallhacks, aimbots, movement scripts and macros are all banned.
  2. No ghosting or stream snipingDon't use a stream, a spectator or a friend to see where the enemy is.
  3. Keep voice and chat cleanNo toxic voice, slurs, spam or abuse in chat.
  4. No griefing teammatesNo blocking, team-flashing or damaging teammates on purpose, and no throwing rounds.
  5. Admins run the matchFollow the admin's call on teams, maps, pauses and disputes.

For every channel and voice room on our Discord.

  1. Stay on topicUse each channel for what its name says.
  2. No spam, self-promo or adsNo floods of messages, invite links or adverts for other servers.
  3. Nothing sexual, gory or upsettingThat includes names and profile pictures.
  4. Don't DM staff about bansUse the appeal form and a ticket instead.
  5. English in public channelsSo everyone, including staff, can follow the conversation.

Questions & answers

Can't find yours? Open a ticket.

How do I join on Java?
Open Minecraft Java Edition (any version from 1.21 to 26.3) and choose Multiplayer, then Add Server. Paste mc.thetitansclan.com as the address, press Done, then Join Server. The full join guide has pictures of each step.
Can I play on Bedrock (phone or console)?
Not yet. Bedrock (phone/console) is coming soon. We'll announce it here and on Discord the day it works. For now you need Minecraft Java Edition on a computer.
How long until my rank arrives?
Ranks are delivered in-game automatically, usually within a minute of paying. If yours doesn't show, leave and rejoin the server. Still missing? Open a Payments ticket with your order number and Minecraft username.
Can I get a refund?
Sometimes. You get a full refund if your item never arrived and we can't fix it, if you were charged twice, or if a child bought something without a parent's permission. Most other purchases are final once used. The refund policy has the details.
What's a season pass?
A season lasts about 3 months. A season pass gives you a rank for the whole season with one payment, and it ends on its own (it doesn't renew). It costs 2.5× the monthly price, so less than paying three months. For example, Squire is ₹99 a month or ₹249 for the season. Compare ranks.
Does AFK pool time count for rewards?
No. Time in the AFK pool does not count toward playtime rewards. Only time you're actually playing does.
I'm under 18. Can I buy things?
Yes, but only with a parent or guardian's permission. If a child buys something without permission, a parent can open a Parents ticket within 14 days for a full refund (the item is removed). Parents can read more on the store's parents section.
How long do appeals take?
There's no fixed time. Staff read appeals in order and reply in your ticket. Opening extra tickets or messaging staff won't make it faster.
